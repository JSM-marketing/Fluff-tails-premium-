YOUR DATA

Privacy policy

How we collect, use and protect your personal information.

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Who we are

This website is run by Flufftails Dog Grooming, a home-based dog grooming salon on Nantgarw Road, Caerphilly. We are the data controller for the personal information described in this policy. You can contact us about your data by calling or texting 07818 729593, or through the enquiry form on our home page.

What information we collect

  • Enquiries: your name, phone number, email address (if given), your dog's name, breed and size, the service you're interested in, your preferred date and anything you write in your message.
  • Bookings: your contact details, appointment details and payment records. Online bookings are made through Goldie, which has its own privacy policy.
  • Information about your dog's health and behaviour that you choose to share, so we can groom them safely.
  • Website analytics (only if you accept): how you use this website, such as the pages you visit, your device and browser and your approximate location, collected through Google Analytics. See our cookie policy.
  • Technical data: our hosting provider automatically records basic information such as IP addresses to keep the website secure and running.

How we use it and our lawful basis

PurposeLawful basis (UK GDPR)
Replying to your enquiry and arranging your dog's groomSteps taken at your request before entering a contract, and performing that contract
Keeping business and financial recordsLegal obligation
Keeping the website secure and preventing spamLegitimate interests
Website analyticsYour consent, which you can withdraw at any time

We do not sell your information, and we will only send you marketing messages if you have agreed to receive them.

Who we share it with

  • Vercel, our website host.
  • Web3Forms, which delivers enquiry form messages to our email inbox.
  • Goldie, our online booking system, if you book online.
  • Google, for Google Analytics (only if you accept analytics cookies) and Google Fonts, which load the fonts on this site and receive your IP address when they do.
  • Meta (WhatsApp and Instagram), if you choose to message us on those apps. Their own privacy policies apply.
  • Our accountant, or authorities where the law requires it.

International transfers

Some of these providers process data outside the UK, including in the United States. Where they do, they protect it with recognised safeguards such as the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the UK International Data Transfer Addendum.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that don't lead to a booking: up to 12 months.
  • Client records: up to 6 years after your last appointment.
  • Financial records: 6 years, as required by HMRC.
  • Analytics data: up to 14 months.

Your rights

You have the right to access the personal information we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, restrict or object to how we use it, ask for a copy in a portable format, and withdraw your consent at any time. To use any of these rights, contact us using the details above. We will reply within one month.

Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please contact us first. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
