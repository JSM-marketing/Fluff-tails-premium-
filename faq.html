Dog grooming FAQs

Everything you need to know before your dog's groom. Can't see your question? Just get in touch.

How much does a dog groom cost?

A full groom for a long coat starts from £45 and a smooth coat from £30. A puppy groom is £25, nail trims are £10 and ear cleans are £5. Full groom prices depend on your dog's size, breed and coat condition, and we'll confirm the price when you book.

How long does a groom take?

Around 2 hours for a long coat full groom, around 1.5 hours for a smooth coat or puppy groom, about 20 minutes for a nail trim and 10 minutes for an ear clean.

What's included in a full groom?

  • Long coat: bath and blow-dry, full body haircut and style, hygiene trim, paws and pads, nail trim and ear clean.
  • Smooth coat: bath and blow-dry, brush and de-shed, nail trim, paw tidy and ear clean.

Do you groom puppies?

Yes. The puppy groom is a gentle introduction to grooming for puppies up to 6 months old, so they learn that grooming is calm and enjoyable from the start.

My dog is nervous or elderly. Can you still groom them?

Yes, nervous and elderly dogs are very welcome. Every dog is groomed one-to-one in a calm home salon, with plenty of time, patience and individual attention. Let us know about any worries, health conditions or mobility issues when you book.

Where are you based?

Flufftails is a home-based salon on Nantgarw Road, opposite Caerphilly Castle (CF83 3FB). You'll get the full address when you book.

When are you open?

Appointments are available 7 days a week, from 10am to 6pm.

How do I book?

You can book online, call or text 07818 729593, WhatsApp, send a message on Instagram or use the enquiry form.

Are you qualified and insured?

Yes. Flufftails is run by a qualified dog groomer who is canine first aid trained and fully insured.

What if I need to cancel or I'm running late?

Please give as much notice as you can if you need to cancel. Late cancellations and no-shows may be charged a fee. If you arrive late, the groom may need to be shortened or rebooked.

How often should my dog be groomed?

As a general guide, long and curly coats are best groomed every 4 to 8 weeks, and smooth coats every 8 to 12 weeks. Nails usually need trimming every 4 to 6 weeks. We'll recommend what suits your dog.

How should I prepare my dog for their groom?

A short walk and a toilet break before you arrive helps your dog settle. Please tell us about any health conditions, skin problems or behaviour we should know about.

Ready to book your dog's groom?

Full grooms from £30. Appointments 7 days a week.
